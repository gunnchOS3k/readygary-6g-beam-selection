# Abstract

ReadyGary: Edge-Efficient Beam Selection for mmWave 6G under Mobility and Blockage presents a **research prototype** artifact for community-scale 6G research. Contribution focus: beam benchmark scaffold: top-k, dB loss, SE loss, latency vs oracle on toy channel.
Results reported here are from **preliminary toy demonstrations** using synthetic data only; field validation is future work.
We do not claim operational 6G deployment, Oulu/CWC affiliation, or carrier-grade performance.

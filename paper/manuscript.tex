\begin{abstract}
This companion artifact supplies the beam-selection action model for Paper~II.
Seeded TDL channels use $f_c=28\times10^9$~Hz, labelled FR2~mmWave after 3GPP~TS~38.101-2~\cite{3gpp_ts38101_2}, not Sub-6.
On held-out draws, exhaustive codebook search is a valid model-oracle for this DFT codebook; hierarchical search trades $1.2082$~dB mean SNR for lower host-process time.
An AoA-informed window and an information-equivalent nearest DFT bin see the same geometry hint and diverge: windowed search is better than nearest-bin, but worse than hierarchical search that ignores the hint on this generator.
DeepMIMO is related methodology~\cite{alkhateeb2019deepmimo} and was not required to produce these tables.
\end{abstract}
